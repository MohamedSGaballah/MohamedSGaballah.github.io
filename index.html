Senior Design.
Real partners. Real challenges.

Connecting student engineering teams with sponsors and practical engineering problems.

As Senior Design Industry Liaison in CMU's School of Engineering & Technology, I build industry partnerships, coordinate sponsor communications, help define project scopes and Statements of Work (SOWs), and connect sponsor needs with student team capabilities.

I have secured and coordinated 16 industry-sponsored Senior Design projects, generating $60,000 for the program and setting a program record for industry engagement. I support a structured design process spanning concept development, prototyping, and testing.
