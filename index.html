Engineering knowledge
with practical purpose.

My work brings together environmental engineering research, university teaching, and hands-on engineering practice.

At Central Michigan University, I teach and mentor students in environmental engineering and support an industry-driven Senior Design program. My approach emphasizes collaborative problem-solving, field experience, case studies, and translating engineering fundamentals into solutions that can be implemented in practice.

My perspective is also shaped by experience owning and operating a factory, which informs how I guide students to consider manufacturability, cost, quality, safety, and operational constraints—not only whether a design works on paper.

Senior Design.
Real partners. Real challenges.

An industry-driven capstone experience connecting student teams with sponsors and practical engineering problems.

As Senior Design Industry Liaison, I help connect organizations with project teams, coordinate sponsor engagement, and support a structured design process. Students work through project definition, concept development, design decisions, prototyping, testing, and communication of results.
