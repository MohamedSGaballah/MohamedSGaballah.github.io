Senior Design Manager

Engineering knowledge
with practical purpose.

My work brings together environmental engineering research, university teaching, and hands-on engineering practice.

At Central Michigan University, I teach and mentor students across core engineering curricula and direct an industry-sponsored Senior Design program. My teaching emphasizes collaborative problem-solving, field visits, laboratory investigation, and translating fundamentals into practical systems.

My perspective is also shaped by founding and managing a manufacturing facility (Cad Cam Technology Co.). That experience informs how I approach design decisions involving manufacturability, cost, quality, workforce coordination, safety, and operational constraints.

My academic training includes a Ph.D. in Engineering from China Agricultural University and M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees from Alexandria University.
