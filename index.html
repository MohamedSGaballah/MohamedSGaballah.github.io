My work brings together environmental engineering research, university teaching, and hands-on engineering practice.

At Central Michigan University, I teach and mentor students in engineering and support an industry-driven Senior Design program. My teaching emphasizes collaborative problem-solving, field experience, case studies, laboratory learning, and the translation of engineering fundamentals into practical solutions.

My perspective is also shaped by founding and managing a manufacturing facility. That experience informs how I approach design decisions involving manufacturability, cost, quality, workforce coordination, safety, and operational constraints.

My academic training includes a Ph.D. in Engineering from China Agricultural University and M.Sc. and B.Sc. degrees from Alexandria University.
